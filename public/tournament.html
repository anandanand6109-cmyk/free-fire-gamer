📜 Rules & Regulations

Organizer should confirm and publish final rules before registrations open.

🎮 General Rules

  1. Mobile devices only. PC emulators are prohibited for this event.
  2. VPN usage is prohibited. Use a stable local internet connection.
  3. Gun attributes must be turned OFF for equal weapon performance, if supported by the selected mode.
  4. Revival settings follow the announced room rules and selected game mode.
  5. Players must enter the lobby on time. Late entry may be refused by the organizer.
  6. Do not use cheats, hacks, exploits or unauthorized third-party tools.
  7. Follow the organizer's instructions and maintain respectful conduct.

🕒 Room & Match Rules

  1. Room ID and password should be shared in the official group or lobby 5–15 minutes before match time.
  2. Players must use their registered in-game name and correct team details.
  3. Check the selected map, mode, team size and room settings before the match.
  4. Report disconnections or disputes to the organizer immediately.
  5. Kill points and placement determine advancement when specified in the event format.
  6. Organizer decisions and dispute procedures should be published before the event.

🏆 Tournament Format

Based on the group-stage format you provided.
01

Group Stage

Four groups play one Battle Royale match each. The top four teams from each group advance.

02

Second Round

Qualified teams from Groups A and B compete; qualified teams from Groups C and D compete. The top two teams in each match advance.

03

Clash Squad Semi-Finals

Teams that advance from the second round compete in Clash Squad matches to decide the finalists.

04

Grand Final

The two finalists play a Clash Squad match to determine the champion.

Important: The group-stage format above is intended for team tournaments. Lone Wolf 1v1 and Duo 2v2 should have their own match brackets. Define tie-breakers, scoring, disqualification, refunds and prize conditions in writing before accepting entries.
